# AmpliWidgets

An ultra-fast, lightweight native **iOS 27 application and interactive widget suite** designed specifically for **Marantz and Denon AV Receivers**.

Stop hunting between couch cushions for plastic remotes or waiting through slow startup screens. AmpliWidgets puts tactile, 1-click receiver controls directly on your **Home Screen, Lock Screen, and Control Center**.

### Key Features
- **4 Interactive iOS 27 Widgets:**
  - **The Contextual Hub:** Dual-zone console with an interactive 1-tap switcher between volume controls and your 4-input launcher.
  - **The Porthole Luxe:** Iconic circular porthole dial with champagne gold accents, live decibel readouts, and stepped volume.
  - **The Direct Studio Deck:** Professional mixing console layout with dedicated vertical volume steppers and source buttons.
  - **The Ultra-Minimal Pill:** Compact, unified HomeKit-style capsule with volume rocker and active streaming indicators.
- **Control Center & Lock Screen Controls:** 1-tap widgets for Main Zone Power, Mute, Volume Up, and Volume Down.
- **Precise Volume & Safety Ceilings:** Real hardware relative decibel stepping (±0.5 dB, ±1.0 dB, or ±2.0 dB), dual volume scales (Relative -dB and Absolute 0–98), and configurable maximum volume safety limits.
- **1-Tap Source Switching & Streaming Detection:** Instant switching between primary inputs (Apple TV, PS5, Apple Music, TV Audio eARC) with automatic detection of AirPlay, HEOS, and Bluetooth streaming sessions.
- **Zero-Config Network Auto-Discovery:** Automated Bonjour / mDNS network discovery connects to your receiver in seconds, with manual IP fallback and a 1-tap factory reset.
- **Privacy First:** 100% on-device communication strictly over your local Wi-Fi network (port 23). Zero user accounts, zero logins, zero tracking, and zero analytics.
